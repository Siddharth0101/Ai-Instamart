## UPI payment: “Approve payment” with nothing to approve · 25 October 2026 (later)

- **Report:** paying with PhonePe in Brave showed “Approve payment”, but nothing appeared in PhonePe or the Swiggy app.
- **Cause (from Swiggy's own guide, [Pay with UPI end-to-end](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/docs/build/recipes/pay-with-upi/) and the [`place_food_order` reference](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/docs/reference/food/place_food_order/)):** a UPI order is created as `PENDING_PAYMENT` and Swiggy pushes no request anywhere. The client must show the payment: Swiggy returns a `upi://` link (to open a UPI app or render as a QR) and a `bridgeUrl` payment page (a QR on desktop, “open in UPI app” on mobile), and on desktop the QR option (`generateUPIQR`) should be used rather than an app intent. SayCart dropped the `upi://` link, showed the payment page only if it was on a swiggy.com address (Swiggy promises no host), offered the PhonePe app intent on a computer, never settled an unpaid order when the payment window closed (the order stayed “waiting” and blocked new orders), ignored Swiggy's cancelled / cart-changed / refund end states, polled every 4 s regardless of Swiggy's ~19 s long poll, and the agent said “Order placed! Approve the PhonePe request on your phone”, which was untrue twice over. The development store copied the same wrong story, so earlier tests passed.
- **Fixed:** the waiting screen now says “Pay to place your order. Not placed yet…”, shows a scannable QR of the `upi://` link on a computer, an **Open UPI app** button on a phone, **Open payment page** whenever Swiggy sends one, and the time left to pay. A computer offers **Scan UPI QR** (and “PhonePe se” chooses it); a phone offers the UPI apps. When the time runs out SayCart calls `confirm_order` once, as Swiggy asks, and the order ends as not placed (a late payment is settled by Swiggy); cancelled, cart-changed and refund states end it too. Status checks run one at a time. The agent's replies can no longer call an unpaid order placed or promise a phone request. The development store now behaves like Swiggy (no request, a `upi://` link, a QR option, unpaid orders fail when confirmed).
- **Checked:** four new scripted tests (computer → QR and the honest reply, then paid → confirmed; phone → the app; time running out → not placed; cancelled → not placed; device-specific options) and `npm test` **246/246**. In the preview on the test store: the computer layout offered Cash, Scan UPI QR and Swiggy Money; placing showed “Pay to place your order”, the QR and “Pay within 4:59”; the simulated payment then moved it to Order confirmed. At phone width (an Android phone as far as the page can tell) the options were Cash, PhonePe UPI and Swiggy Money; placing showed **Open UPI app** (a `upi://pay…` link) with no QR, the time left and the header chip “Pay to place order”, with no sideways scrolling; after the simulated payment the order went on to “Preparing”. After the last wording changes, `npm test` **246/246** and the typecheck passed again. No real payment was attempted.
- **The order behind the Brave report** (found with permission, with the real app stopped so its local store could be read): there was no restaurant order at all. The only stuck order was an **Instamart UPI checkout from 18 October**, still marked “payment pending” a week later and holding the order lock. Swiggy's UPI window is minutes, and this old flow never stored the payment reference that `check_payment_status` / `confirm_order` need, so it was closed locally as not placed and its lock removed.
