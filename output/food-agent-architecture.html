GroupExample options to confirmQuantity rule
Adults / teensVeg: paneer + rice/roti; non-veg: chicken + rice/roti. Cuisine, spice and appetite are user choices.Use the chosen meal’s merchant-listed serving coverage.
ChildrenAsk familiar foods and spice preference. If requested: mild veg rice, idli, or simple sandwiches; offer the shared menu too.Use caregiver-confirmed appetite; no automatic child meal stereotype.
Younger childrenCaregiver selects suitable familiar foods individually.Do not infer age-appropriate foods or automatic bulk portions.
Everyone / suppliesAsk about water/drinks, dessert, starters, plates, cups and napkins; subtract supplies already at home.Extras are opt-in; count total guests once; avoid duplicate sides.
Blocking questions before menu execution
Veg / non-veg split confirmed? Jain / vegan / allergies or cross-contact concerns? Exact event date, address and serve-by time? Dinner only, or starters + dinner? Anything already available?
Portions above are planning assumptions, not pack counts. Shared mains are counted together; merchant servings must be checked before converting to cart quantities.
Execution limits: Swiggy Food’s current guide documents a ₹1,000 Builders Club cart cap and immediate delivery. A larger party plan can require a catering / direct-provider handoff. Never split purchases to evade a cap; no guaranteed synchronized arrivals.
